# Community code of conduct

Nexus welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and code rather than
people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, impersonate, or expose
private information about another person. Do not use project spaces to promote
abuse or illegal harm.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Enforcement
decisions should consider context, severity, repeated behavior, and the safety
of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository
host. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. No one
should face retaliation for making a good-faith report.
